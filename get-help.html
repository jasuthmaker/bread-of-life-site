How our pantry works…

We are a shopping model pantry. You choose your own food. We have volunteers who help you shop and carry your food to your car.

We are not able to deliver food. However, many of our members car pool with neighbors and friends. We are happy to help schedule appointment times so your car pool can shop at the same time.

What we stock…

  • Canned goods
  • Boxed dry goods
  • Prepared foods
  • Dairy
  • Frozen meats
  • Produce
  • Bread
  • Bakery items
  • Snacks
  • Seasonal goods
  • Personal care/cleaning items

Our stock is rotated out and constantly changing. During the growing season, we offer a wide variety of locally raised produce. We typically do not stock infant formula or baby food.

What we do not do…

Unfortunately we cannot provide any of the following: financial assistance, rental assistance, utility assistance, or help with holiday gifts. You can dial 211 to get connected with resources that may be able to help.

Frequently asked questions

When are you open?

We are open Tuesdays 12:30 to 4:30 p.m. and Wednesdays 10 a.m. to 2 p.m.

Do you deliver?

No. We just do not have the capacity to do that right now.

Do you accept walk-ins?

No. We ask that you make an appointment, because we are a small space with a small parking lot. Call 502-228-0650 to set up an appointment.

Isn’t it better to come on Tuesdays?

No. We receive fresh foods throughout the week. We also bring foods in from cold storage throughout both pantry days at various times. This gives everyone a fair chance to receive fresh foods no matter what time your appointment is. We’d love to see you on Wednesdays.

Where do I go?

We are located inside Grace & Glory at 11952 W. Hwy 42 in Goshen, Kentucky. It’s the little white church on the hill with red doors. Come inside the blue side doors and our volunteers will greet you.

Do I need to bring any documents?

No. We just ask basic information so that we can report to Dare To Care.

Do I need to bring my own bags?

No. We have plenty.

What about bad weather?

See all the details in Inclement Weather above.

Do you provide rental assistance?

No. Unfortunately this is beyond what we do. You can dial 211 to get connected with resources that may be able to help.

Do you provide help with holiday gifts?

No. This is also unfortunately beyond what we do. You can dial 211 for resources.

Do you have pet food?

Sometimes. We occasionally receive small amounts of pet food based on what retailers donate.

Do you have baby supplies?

No. We sometimes have small amounts of baby food and snacks. We do not carry formula.

What type of items do you have?

We have canned goods, pantry basics, personal care and cleaning items, boxed foods, cereal, deli items, cheeses, fresh produce, individual serving meals, frozen meats, bread, and bakery items. We sometimes have eggs and milk. We also have small, fun seasonal items.

What if I have an appointment and can’t make it?

Please call us as soon as you know at 502-228-0650. If we don’t answer, please leave a message. If someone can pick up for you, we are happy to pack a box if you let us know ahead of time.
