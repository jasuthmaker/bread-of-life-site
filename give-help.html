Food Donation Drop-Off

Donations of canned goods and other shelf-stable food can be made at our pantry. Small amounts can be left in the bin outside Bread of Life food pantry, 11952 W. Hwy 42, Goshen, Ky.

Donors can make arrangements for larger amounts by calling the pantry at (502) 228-0650, or emailing kyfoodpantry@gmail.com.

What we accept

Bread of Life is strictly a food pantry. We only accept food and hygiene products. We DO NOT accept donations of clothing, household goods and similar items.

Volunteer

We are grateful for the dozens of volunteers who operate Bread of Life food pantry. We have several volunteer jobs available. Hours and days vary. Those younger than 16 cannot volunteer when clients are shopping.

  • Stocker

    Keep our shelves, refrigerators and freezers stocked and organized.

  • Shopper

    Help our clients select food from our shelves.

  • Check in

    Using our computer, check in shoppers and schedule appointments.

  • Pick up

    Using your personal vehicle, travel to partner stores to pick up food.

To volunteer, contact pantry manager Susan Kreps at (502) 228-0650 or email kyfoodpantry@gmail.com.

Questions? We have answers.

Do you accept walk-ins?

No. We ask that you make an appointment, because we are a small space with a small parking lot. Call 502-228-0650 to set up an appointment.

Do I need to bring any documents?

No. We just ask basic information so that we can report to Dare To Care.

Where do I go?

We are located inside Grace & Glory at 11952 W. Hwy 42 in Goshen, Kentucky. It’s the little white church on the hill with red doors. Come inside the blue side doors and our volunteers will greet you.

Do you deliver?

No. We just do not have the capacity to do that right now.

Isn’t it better to come on Tuesdays?

No. We receive fresh foods throughout the week. We also bring foods in from cold storage throughout both pantry days at various times. This gives everyone a fair chance to receive fresh foods no matter what time your appointment is. We’d love to see you on Wednesdays.
